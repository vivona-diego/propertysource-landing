# Property Source Exchange

Property Source Exchange is a global real estate exchange platform owned and operated by Property Hub Exchange, Inc. It connects independent branded real estate marketplaces into one shared network for property discovery, digital transactions, lenders, title companies, and related services.

## Create a free branded marketplace

A real estate community, network, lender, loan broker, brokerage, influencer, educator, asset manager, or other real estate organization can launch a turnkey white-label marketplace with its own custom domain, logo, colors, and branded registration. The marketplace has no setup fee, no monthly platform fee, and no out-of-pocket platform payment.

An organization may also connect an existing marketplace through a two-way API integration. Each marketplace keeps its own brand and members while receiving access to inventory and services available across the Property Source Exchange network.

## Connected exchange network

The official website currently identifies 480 connected marketplaces, 14,000 integrated title companies, and 21 integrated lenders. These counts may change as the network grows.

Connected marketplaces share aggregated on-market and off-market property inventory while remaining independently branded. Supported asset classes include residential, multifamily, commercial, industrial, land, and mortgage-note assets.

## Platform features

### Marketplace network

- Turnkey white-label marketplace with a custom domain, logo, colors, and registration
- Two-way API connection for existing websites and marketplaces
- Aggregated inventory from connected independent marketplaces
- Single sign-on for a worldwide view of available deals
- Residential, multifamily, commercial, industrial, land, and mortgage-note listings

### Discovery and privacy

- Individual property listing and bulk CSV or XML uploads
- Map, satellite, Street View, and address-assisted property search
- AI-assisted U.S. property research through text or voice
- Blind-sale privacy protecting buyer and seller identities until signing
- Email and text notifications for matching property opportunities

### Digital transactions and closing

- Digital offer generation, submission, negotiation, acceptance, and rejection
- Contract generation and electronic signatures
- Automated transaction, document, closing, and post-closing workflows
- Accepted-deal handoff into title-company workflows
- Online earnest-money deposits to title-company escrow accounts
- Real-time closing milestone tracking
- Integrated lenders, title companies, inspections, surveys, insurance, attorneys, and transaction resources
- Online property auctions with marketplace-controlled pricing and duration

### Operations and communications

- Marketplace-owner dashboard for members, activity, listings, transactions, settings, reports, revenue, and payments
- Branded email and SMS alerts, campaigns, announcements, and invitations
- Owner selection of lending services and participating providers
- Unlimited owner-controlled banner advertisements and affiliate placements

## Revenue-share model

Property Source Exchange supplies and manages the technology and transaction infrastructure. The marketplace owner promotes the marketplace to its community and receives evergreen revenue from qualifying activity generated by that network.

### Buyer transactions

A buyer pays a $1,500 technology fee for platform technology, offer tools, transaction coordination, transaction management, infrastructure, and related digital purchase services. The marketplace receives 20%, equal to $300 per qualifying buyer transaction.

### Seller transactions

A seller may list an unlimited number of properties. The seller pays a $500 technology fee for property data, comparable-sales tools, online contracting, portfolio management, electronic contracts, e-signatures, and related services. The fee is paid at settlement and disbursed by the title company. The marketplace receives 20%, equal to $100 per qualifying seller transaction.

### Advertising and affiliate income

Marketplace owners may add unlimited banner advertisements and affiliate placements. The marketplace owner controls them through the owner dashboard and retains 100% of the advertising and affiliate revenue.

### Tracking and evergreen payments

Qualifying transaction fees, settlements, activity, and marketplace payments are tracked by the platform and visible in the owner dashboard. The marketplace’s revenue participation continues on qualifying transactions generated by its network.
