Property Source Exchange

Create your marketplace. Give your members access to property deals around the world.

Property Source Exchange is a global real estate exchange operated by Property Hub Exchange, Inc. It connects independent branded marketplaces into one shared property, services, and transaction network.

Free turnkey white-label marketplaces

Marketplace partners receive a custom-branded marketplace with no setup fee and no monthly platform fee. Partners may use a custom domain, logo, colors, owner dashboard, branded communications, and integrated property and transaction services.

Connected platform capabilities

The platform includes aggregated listings, multiple asset types, map-powered search, bulk uploads, digital offers and contracts, negotiation, electronic signatures, online earnest money, title-company handoff, closing tracking, lenders, transaction vendors, property auctions, member management, reporting, email, SMS, advertising, and affiliate tools.

Evergreen marketplace revenue

A qualifying buyer transaction carries a $1,500 technology fee, with 20% or $300 allocated to the marketplace. A qualifying seller transaction carries a $500 technology fee paid at settlement, with 20% or $100 allocated to the marketplace. Marketplace owners also control unlimited banner and affiliate advertising and retain 100% of that advertising revenue. Activity and payments are tracked in the marketplace-owner dashboard.
