Property Source Exchange

Create your marketplace. Give your members access to property deals around the world.

Property Source Exchange is a global real estate exchange operated by Property Hub Exchange, Inc. It connects independent branded marketplaces into one shared property, services, and transaction network.

Free turnkey white-label marketplaces

Marketplace partners receive a custom-branded marketplace with no setup fee and no monthly platform fee. Partners may use a custom domain, logo, colors, owner dashboard, branded communications, and integrated property and transaction services.

Earn Revenue — Your community transacts. Your marketplace earns.

Connect your existing marketplace or launch a branded one with the turnkey templates. No setup fees, no monthly platform fees, and no out-of-pocket platform costs.

Every marketplace can host both buyers and sellers.

The owner of the independent marketplace hosting the seller receives 20% of the $500 seller technology fee: $100. The owner of the independent marketplace hosting the buyer receives 20% of the $1,500 buyer technology fee: $300.

Every marketplace can host both buyers and sellers; these are transaction roles, not marketplace types. If one marketplace hosts both members of a completed sale, its owner receives $100 + $300 = $400. If different marketplaces bring the seller and buyer, the seller’s marketplace receives $100 and the buyer’s marketplace receives $300. The share follows the member. These percentages apply to technology fees, not property sale prices. Shares arise from completed transactions, not simply a listing or offer. The seller technology fee is paid at settlement and disbursed by the title company.

Ambassador program — 5%

Earn an ongoing 5% ambassador share from transaction activity generated by each new independent marketplace you refer to the exchange.

Free built-in banner advertising

Post unlimited ads for vendors, services, affiliates, or anything you choose to promote. Ads are displayed to your own marketplace’s members. Manage them in your owner dashboard with full control and no platform charge or advertising revenue share; retain 100% of your advertising and affiliate revenue.

Evergreen, not a one-time reward.

Transaction and ambassador revenue shares continue while the relevant marketplaces remain active and transacting. Transactions and revenue shares are tracked in the owner dashboard.
